03 / Trivia

Taco Trivia

Tacos have been documented for centuries, and the format — a wrapped, portable meal — is basically the original semantic element: a container built exactly for its content. Click a fact below to reveal it.

Did you know? 0/3 opened

Where does "taco" come from?

Historians trace the word to Mexican silver miners, who used "tacos" to describe small explosive charges wrapped in paper — a shape later borrowed for food.

What's the tortilla's job?

It's structural, not just flavor — a good tortilla holds everything together without tearing, the edible equivalent of a well-nested HTML container.

Biggest taco ever made?

Teams in Mexico have built tacos measured in dozens of meters long, assembled by entire towns for festival records.
